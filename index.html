Privacy Policy

PDF & Word Converter

Effective date: October 23, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how PDF & Word Converter ("the App") handles documents and information when you use its PDF and Word conversion features. We aim to handle information responsibly and explain our practices clearly.

1. Information We Process

When you use the App, you may select and upload PDF or Word documents for conversion. These documents may contain personal or confidential information. Please upload only files you are authorized to process.

The App processes the documents you submit to provide the conversion service. Technical information may also be processed by the server or hosting provider to deliver the service, maintain reliability, and troubleshoot errors.

2. How We Use Your Documents

Documents are processed to:

  • Convert Word documents into PDF files.
  • Convert PDF documents into Word files.
  • Make converted documents available for download.
  • Maintain and troubleshoot the conversion service.
  • Protect the service against abuse and technical problems.

3. Temporary File Storage and Deletion

Documents and converted files may be temporarily stored on our conversion server while the conversion and download process is completed.

The service is intended to delete temporary conversion files automatically after approximately 15 minutes. Actual retention depends on the deployed server configuration. Users should download converted files promptly and should not treat the service as permanent file storage.

4. Sharing of Information

Documents are transmitted to the conversion backend because this processing is necessary to provide the requested service. Our hosting or infrastructure providers may process technical information as needed to operate and secure the service.

We do not sell users' documents or personal information. Any additional sharing with third-party services, such as advertising or analytics providers, must be disclosed in this policy if those services are used by the App.

5. Advertising and Analytics

If advertising or analytics services are integrated into the App, those providers may collect information such as device identifiers, app interactions, diagnostics, or advertising data, depending on the services and their configuration. Their collection and use of information are governed by their applicable privacy policies.

If the App does not use advertising or analytics services, this section should be updated to accurately reflect that fact.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information processed by the service. However, no method of transmission or electronic storage is completely secure, and absolute security cannot be guaranteed.

Please avoid uploading highly sensitive documents unless you are comfortable with the service's processing and storage practices.

7. Data Retention

Documents are retained only as long as needed for the conversion and download process, subject to the server's actual configuration. Technical logs, if maintained, may be retained for operational, security, or troubleshooting purposes for the period necessary for those purposes.

8. Children's Privacy

The App is not specifically designed to collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the service, please contact us so we can review the matter.

9. Your Choices and Responsibilities

  • You choose which documents to upload for conversion.
  • You should ensure you have permission to process the documents you submit.
  • You should save downloaded files in a location you control.
  • You can stop using the App at any time.

10. Third-Party Services and Links

The App may rely on hosting, infrastructure, or other service providers to operate. External websites and services have their own privacy practices. Where applicable, review the policies of those providers for further information.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy
